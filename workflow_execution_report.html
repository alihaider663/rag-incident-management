Cognitive RAG Intelligence

Multi-stage reasoning using OpenAI embeddings and GPT-4o. Automatically clusters and classifies incidents into P1-P4 tiers, assesses business impact, and extracts verified root-causes from historical runbooks.

Fail-Safe Governance & Human-in-the-Loop

Keyword-driven risk classification. Low-risk operations (e.g. cache clear) auto-approve; high-risk actions (e.g. database drop/restart) pause execution and notify executive approvers with two-phase verification endpoints.

Closed-Loop Continuous Feedback

Resolved incident post-mortems are automatically converted to knowledge articles. Cosine similarity duplicate detection (>0.95 threshold) ensures vector store hygiene and continually expands operational memory.
